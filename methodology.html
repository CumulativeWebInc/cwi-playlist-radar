Methodology

How the Playlist Fraud Radar grades playlists — and what it refuses to do.

The tiers

V — Verified. An actual placement add was confirmed by a full playlist scan or engine collect on the stated date. This is the strongest grade we issue.
U — Unverified claim. A curator claimed an add or promised one; CWI could not confirm it. This is not evidence of fraud — it means exactly what it says.
P — Paid-placement pattern (factual). The curator publicly advertises paid placement. This is a cited fact about their business model, not an accusation of wrongdoing. Artists should read the terms before paying.

Tiers, never verdicts

We grade evidence, not people. A wrong flag can harm a legitimate curator's livelihood, so this dashboard will never call anyone a scammer or fraudster. Every automated build runs a defamation guard that fails closed on verdict language. Flags must cite their sources; a flag without evidence is rejected by the validator before it can ship.

Freshness

A grade is fresh only if its evidence was observed within the last 48 hours. Older grades are labeled stale. Stale is honest. We would rather show you a stale grade than manufacture a fresh-looking one — a validator enforces this on every build.

Limits

  • This is CWI's verified view of the playlists we have investigated, seeded from our own campaign scans. It is not a universal authority.
  • A playlist not listed here has no grade from us at all — absence from the radar is not a grade.
  • Evidence types we accept: full playlist scans, paginated engine collects, direct review of a curator's public site, documented failed verification. Rumors, screenshots from strangers, and vibes are not evidence.
  • The seed set is small (5 playlists). It grows only when a new verification event happens.

Corrections

If you are a curator and an entry is wrong, email hp@cumulativeweb.com with evidence. We will re-scan and update the entry. Corrections are logged in the entry's notes — not hidden.

Machine-readable data

The full dataset is radar.json — same tiers, same evidence, same freshness. The validator that guards it (validator.js) and its adversarial test suite (test.js) ship in the repo alongside the data.
